Capriole AI costs **USD 8 a month** for unlimited web chat with **GPT-6 Astra**, **Claude Fable 5.1**, **Claude Opus 5.5**, and **Gemini 4 Argon**. Capriole AI also includes an API that costs up to 92% less than official API prices, so the same account runs your apps and coding agents. This comparison covers published plans and features, not a test of answer quality.

## Switching models in one conversation

Changing models halfway through a task is the main reason to use one platform instead of several apps. In Capriole AI you pick another model from the menu and the next answer comes from it, in the same thread, with saved files carried over when the new model and storage mode support them. [Switch models mid-chat](/articles/switch-models-mid-chat) explains what carries over and what Capriole AI adapts between vendors' formats.

Magai's pricing page also lists switching models mid-chat without losing the thread. T3 Chat's official feedback board marks conversation branching and a keyboard model switcher as completed; its [comparison with Capriole AI](/articles/capriole-ai-vs-t3-chat) covers the rest of its workspace.

## Privacy and where your chats are kept

Every platform here sends a frontier-model request to the vendor that makes the model, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google, or to a cloud platform that hosts it. What differs is training, logging, and where your history lives.

| | Training on your content | Where chat history is kept | What else is kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capriole AI | Not used to train AI models | Your choice: your account (default), only your browser, or not saved | The API keeps no request or response content, only usage records such as token counts |
| Magai | Says your work never trains a model | On Magai's servers while your account is active | Its servers and service providers are in the United States |
| T3 Chat | Does not train its own models; opted out of provider training where possible | Not stated in its FAQ | With your own provider key, your provider's settings apply |
| Venice | Not stated on the pages we checked | On your device | Requests are relayed without being stored; for frontier models, Venice says to assume the provider stores your content |
| OpenRouter | Says it does not train on your data | Chatroom conversations stay on your device and do not sync | Request metadata such as model and token counts; prompts are not logged unless you opt in, but on Safety Retention endpoints OpenRouter or the provider may keep them to prevent abuse |

Capriole AI's **Local Storage** and **No Storage** modes keep history out of our database, but uploads and image creation need **Server Storage**. The [privacy and data controls guide](/guides/privacy-and-security) lists what is kept in each mode, and the [chat storage guide](/guides/chat-storage) shows how to switch.

## What you give up

Capriole AI is built for individuals and small teams. Magai has built-in agents, scheduled task runs, and integrations with other apps, and Magai and Venice generate video; Capriole AI has no scheduled tasks, app integrations, or video generation. Enterprise controls such as SAML single sign-on are not offered either; [LLM gateway security risks](/articles/llm-gateway-security) lists what Capriole AI does and does not provide. Web chat is unlimited, but API requests and coding agents use a metered balance: 5M tokens a month on Premium, then USD 8 per 5M tokens.
