The best AI subscription under USD 20 depends on whether you want one vendor or all of them. Capriole AI Premium costs **USD 9 a month** for unlimited web chat with all four current flagships, **GPT-6 Astra**, **Claude Fable 5.1**, **Claude Opus 5.5**, and **Gemini 4 Argon**, while ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, and Google AI Pro cost USD 19.99 to USD 20 for one vendor's models each.

Premium also includes 5M API tokens a month. ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro include no API usage. All prices are the US monthly prices on the providers’ own pages.

## Which plan fits which person

- **You want the best model from every vendor for one price.** Capriole AI Premium is the plan in this list that covers GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon in one subscription, for less than half the price of the USD 20 plans. [Switch models mid-chat](/articles/switch-models-mid-chat) to get a second answer from another vendor in the same thread.
- **You also build apps or use coding agents.** Capriole AI Premium's API allowance works with the OpenAI and Anthropic API formats and [10 documented coding agents](/guides/one-api-key-coding-agents); the API column shows what the other plans include.
- **You only need everyday chat.** ChatGPT's free plan may be enough. ChatGPT Go costs USD 8. Capriole AI Premium costs USD 9 and includes the four flagships.

## What USD 9 gets you on Capriole AI

Premium is unlimited web chat on every supported model, including GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon. Signed-in web chat has a fair-use limit of 200 messages per account per day. It stops scripted, automated abuse; normal use stays well below it.

Premium includes 5M API tokens a month. Personal top-ups cost USD 8 per 5M tokens of any supported model, up to 92% below official API prices in the [standard-rate comparison](/overview). Files and web search, projects, chat export, and storage controls are part of the workspace, and free image generation with GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2.1 is also included. [Plans and limits](/guides/plans-and-limits) lists everything in Free, Premium, and Team.

## What you give up

Capriole AI is an independent workspace, not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google, so features that exist only in their apps stay there, such as memory, connectors to other apps, their desktop and mobile apps, and Google AI Pro's 5 TB of storage and Gemini in Gmail, Docs, and Sheets. Capriole AI also has no voice conversation mode and no video or music generation. Coding agents use the metered API balance, not unlimited chat, so heavy agent work costs USD 8 per 5M tokens beyond the included allowance.
