## How Anthropic describes Claude Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5

Anthropic says Claude Fable 5.1 brings stronger long-running agentic coding, multistep research, and document, spreadsheet, and slide work. It is the slower of the two.

For most workloads, Anthropic recommends starting with Claude Opus 5.5. Its announcement says Opus 5.5 performs at the level of Fable 5.1 on most work and, on Anthropic's benchmarks, leads in agentic coding, computer use, and knowledge work. Anthropic suggests Fable 5.1 when Opus 5.5 at higher effort still falls short.

Both models have thinking always on. They read text and images, write text, and have a 1M-token context window.

## How OpenAI describes GPT-6 Astra

OpenAI calls GPT-6 Astra its most capable model for the most demanding work. It lists complex reasoning, coding, computer use, research, and document creation as the main uses. You can set reasoning effort from `low` to `max`.

GPT-6 Astra reads text and images and writes text. On the API, prompts above 272K input tokens cost more: 2× the input rate and 1.5× the output rate for the whole request.

## What each subscription gives you

**Claude.** Every Claude plan has a five-hour session limit, and paid plans add a weekly limit. Claude Pro gives at least 5× the Free plan's usage per session. Max 5x and Max 20x give 5× or 20× Pro's usage per session. On Pro, Fable 5.1 is not part of the plan's usage limits and is billed through usage credits at standard API rates. On Max, Fable models can use up to 50% of the weekly limits. Read [Claude usage limits explained](/articles/claude-usage-limits) for the details.

**ChatGPT.** On Plus, GPT-6 in Chat runs on GPT-6 Sol. GPT-6 Astra is included in ChatGPT Work and Codex, where OpenAI estimates 5 to 45 local messages per five-hour window and says weekly limits may also apply. GPT-6 Pro, the Chat option powered by Astra, needs Pro, Business, or Enterprise. Even on Pro, OpenAI says GPT-6 Pro use is not unlimited. The [ChatGPT Plus alternative](/articles/chatgpt-plus-alternative) page compares Plus with Capriole AI in full.

Neither Claude Pro nor ChatGPT Plus includes API usage. On those plans, both vendors bill the API separately.

## Which one should you use?

- **Start with Claude Opus 5.5** for most coding and knowledge work. That is Anthropic's own advice, and its API price is the lowest of the three.
- **Move to Claude Fable 5.1** for the hardest reasoning or long agentic tasks, when Opus 5.5 is not enough.
- **Use GPT-6 Astra** for demanding reasoning, research, coding, and document work, or for a second opinion from another vendor.

The vendors' benchmarks do not tell you which model is best at your task. Asking two of them in the same thread does.
