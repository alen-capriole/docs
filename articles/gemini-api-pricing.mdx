From December 1, 2026, Argon costs **USD 4 and USD 20**. With three input tokens for every output token, 5M tokens cost USD 20 at the introductory price and USD 40 from December 1 at Google's standard rates.

## Gemini API prices per 1M tokens

Google publishes these paid-tier rates on its [Gemini Developer API pricing page](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). All prices are in USD per 1M tokens, and output prices include thinking tokens.

| Model | API model ID | Period | Input | Cached input | Output |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gemini 4 Argon | `gemini-4-argon` | Through November 30, 2026 | USD 2 | USD 0.10 | USD 10 |
| Gemini 4 Argon | `gemini-4-argon` | From December 1, 2026 | USD 4 | USD 0.20 | USD 20 |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | `gemini-3.8-flash` | Through December 31, 2026 | USD 0.75 | USD 0.075 | USD 3.75 |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | `gemini-3.8-flash` | From January 1, 2027 | USD 1.50 | USD 0.15 | USD 7.50 |

The same page lists the rules that change these rates:

- **Batch and Flex**: Both cost half of standard rates. Gemini 4 Argon costs USD 1 input and USD 5 output through November 30, then USD 2 and USD 10.
- **Priority**: Gemini 4 Argon costs USD 3.60 input and USD 18 output through November 30, then USD 7.20 and USD 36.
- **Caching**: Gemini 4 Argon supports implicit caching only, on by default for prompts of at least 4,096 tokens. Explicit caching is not supported.
- **Free tier**: Gemini 3.8 Flash has a free tier. Gemini 4 Argon does not.
- **Grounding with Google Search**: 5,000 search requests a month are free, shared across Gemini 3 and newer models, then USD 14 per 1,000 requests.

Google lists no separate long-prompt rate for either model.

## What 1M and 5M tokens cost

These examples use standard rates, no caching, and three input tokens for every output token.

```text
official_cost = input_millions × input_price + output_millions × output_price
```

Google prices output, including thinking tokens, at five times input. Capriole counts input and output at one rate, so your own mix decides the saving. For example, 5M input tokens alone cost USD 10 on Argon at the introductory price and USD 20 from December 1.

## How caching and batch change the comparison

- **Cached input**: Google charges USD 0.10 per 1M cached tokens on Argon through November 30 and USD 0.20 from December 1. Capriole counts reported cached input at 10%, which works out to USD 0.16 per 1M cached tokens. Google's cache rate is lower until November 30; Capriole's is lower from December 1. [Charged tokens explained](/articles/charged-tokens) shows the formula.
- **Batch and Flex**: At half price, the 5M-token Argon example costs USD 10 through November 30 and USD 20 from December 1. Capriole has no batch endpoint, and its USD 8 is still 20% and 60% less for that workload.

## Call Gemini 4 Argon through Capriole's API

Capriole serves Gemini models through OpenAI Chat Completions at `POST /v1/chat/completions`. Set the OpenAI SDK base URL to `https://api.caprioletech.com/v1`.

- `google-latest` selects Gemini 4 Argon, so your configuration follows the current recommended Google model.
- `google/gemini-4-argon` and `google/gemini-3.8-flash` select a model explicitly.
- The Responses and Messages endpoints do not accept Gemini models.

```python
import os

from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    api_key=os.environ["CAPRIOLE_AI_API_KEY"],
    base_url="https://api.caprioletech.com/v1",
)

completion = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="google-latest",
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Explain this stack trace."}],
)
print(completion.choices[0].message.content)
```

For long reasoning, stream the response with `stream=True`; without streaming, a request can time out after about 2 minutes. Each key allows 300 requests per minute. See the [Chat Completions reference](/api-reference/endpoint/chat-completions) for every accepted model ID, and [Which AI model to use](/guides/choose-a-model) to compare Gemini 4 Argon with GPT-6 Astra and Claude.
