## Price for flagship chat

Magai measures every plan in dollars of usage. Standard costs USD 20 a month and includes USD 20 of usage; Pro costs USD 40 and includes USD 40; Ultra costs USD 200 and includes USD 200. Magai says how far that goes depends on the models and tools you pick. When you reach the limit, you upgrade or top up in USD 5, 10, or 20 steps. Yearly billing saves 17%.

Magai's Standard plan covers standard models only. Its premium models, which it describes as the top GPT and Claude tiers and anything that costs 3 times or more to run than a standard model, need Pro or Ultra.

Capriole Premium costs USD 8 a month and has no usage meter on web chat. GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, and every other supported model are unlimited. Before you pay, you can chat without an account, 5 messages a day, or sign up for a free account with 500,000 tokens of web chat a month.

## Models

Capriole's current flagship set is GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon. It also serves GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Grok 4.7, GLM 5.3 Flash, and Kimi K3.1 Pro.

Magai's models page lists chat models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, and others, including GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, and Grok 4.7. Its Claude models are Claude Haiku 5.5, Claude Opus 5.5, and Claude Sonnet 5.5, so Claude Fable 5.1 is not on the list. Magai also offers image and video models. Capriole does not generate video.

## API and coding agents

Magai's FAQ says it calls each provider's API on your behalf, so you need no provider account. Its pricing, models, and FAQ pages do not document a developer API you can call from your own code.

Capriole includes one. A single API key works with OpenAI Chat Completions, OpenAI Responses, and Anthropic Messages, up to 92% less than official API prices. Premium includes 5M API tokens a month, and more cost USD 8 per 5M tokens. The [Claude API pricing](/articles/claude-api-pricing) and [OpenAI API pricing](/articles/openai-api-pricing) pages show the math.

The same key powers [10 documented coding agents](/articles/coding-agent-compatibility), including [Claude Code](/integrations/claude-code) and [Codex](/integrations/codex). Agent requests use the API balance, not unlimited chat.

## Workspace features

Magai is built around agents and automation. It has built-in and custom agents, scheduled task runs (100 a month on Standard, 1,000 on Pro, unlimited on Ultra), 150+ integrations, MCP connections, a Canvas document editor, and import from ChatGPT and Claude.

Capriole focuses on chat and the API. Signed-in accounts get free image generation with GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2.1 in Server Storage mode, plus files and web search, [projects](/guides/projects), chat export, and personalization.

## Privacy and storage

Magai's FAQ says your prompts, files, inputs, and outputs are never used to train any model. Its privacy policy says your chats, images, and files are stored while your account is active, and that its servers are in the United States.

Capriole does not use your chats, files, or API content to train AI models, as its [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) states. You choose where chat history lives: synced to your account, kept in your browser, or not stored at all. The [chat storage guide](/guides/chat-storage) shows the settings. Requests in every mode still pass through Capriole and the selected model provider.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Capriole AI cheaper than Magai?

Yes. Capriole Premium costs USD 8 a month for unlimited chat on every supported model. Magai's cheapest plan with the top GPT and Claude tiers is Pro at USD 40 a month, which includes USD 40 of usage.

### Does Magai have Claude Fable 5.1?

Magai's models page does not list it. Its Claude models are Claude Haiku 5.5, Claude Opus 5.5, and Claude Sonnet 5.5. Capriole offers Claude Fable 5.1 in unlimited chat and through its API.

### Does Magai have an API?

Magai's pricing, models, and FAQ pages do not document a developer API. Capriole offers OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs with Premium or Team.

### Which is better for images and video?

Magai, if you need video. Capriole offers free image generation with GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2.1 for signed-in accounts and does not generate video.
