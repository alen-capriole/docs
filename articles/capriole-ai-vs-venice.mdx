## Privacy

Privacy is Venice's main focus. Its docs describe four modes, set per model:

- **Anonymized**: Venice does not send your identity to the model provider, but the provider still sees the prompt. Venice's API pricing lists its Claude, GPT, and Gemini models in this mode.
- **Private**: Prompts and responses are processed for inference only and not retained.
- **TEE**: Supported models run inside hardware-isolated enclaves.
- **E2EE**: Your client encrypts the prompt, and only a verified enclave decrypts it.

Venice says it does not store or log prompt and response content for normal inference. Its pricing page lists conversation history stored locally, with encrypted backup and restore on Pro.

Capriole does not use your chats, files, or API content to train AI models, as its [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) states. You choose where chat history lives: synced to your account, kept in your browser, or not stored at all. The [chat storage guide](/guides/chat-storage) shows the settings. Requests in every mode still pass through Capriole and the selected model provider.

For frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google, both products send your prompt to that provider. Venice's stronger guarantees apply to the open-source models it runs in Private, TEE, or E2EE mode.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Capriole AI cheaper than Venice?

For frontier models, yes. Capriole Premium costs USD 8 a month for unlimited chat with all four current flagships. Venice Pro costs USD 18 and puts frontier text models on credits, with 100 credits, or USD 1, a month.

### Is Venice's API cheaper than official prices?

Not for these flagships. Venice lists Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra at USD 10 input and USD 50 output per 1M tokens, the same as Anthropic and OpenAI. Capriole charges USD 8 per 5M tokens of any supported model, up to 92% less.

### Which is more private?

Venice, for open-source models in Private, TEE, or E2EE mode. For Claude, GPT, and Gemini, both send your prompt to the model provider. Capriole lets you keep no stored chat history and does not train on your content.

### Does Venice support Claude Code?

Yes, with an add-on. Venice's guide installs claude-code-router to convert Claude Code's Anthropic Messages requests into its OpenAI-compatible format. Capriole supports Anthropic Messages directly, so Claude Code needs only its own settings. See the [Claude Code setup](/integrations/claude-code).
