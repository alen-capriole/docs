Yes, you can chat with **GPT-6 Astra**, **Claude Fable 5.1**, **Claude Opus 5.5**, and **Gemini 4 Argon** without creating an account. Open [Capriole AI](https://www.capriole.ai/), choose a model, and send up to 5 messages a day as a guest. A free account adds 500,000 tokens of web chat a month, and Premium costs **USD 8 a month** for unlimited web chat.

Premium also includes an API that costs up to 92% less than official API prices, so the same account can run your apps and coding agents.

## Start a chat without an account

1. Open [www.capriole.ai](https://www.capriole.ai/).
2. Open the model menu and choose a model. **More models** lists the rest.
3. Type in **Ask anything** and send.
4. To keep the chat, select **Log In** and sign in or sign up in the same tab.

The chat then opens as a saved chat in your account, with every message in place. Saving it does not count toward your usage.

## What a free account adds

A free account turns the daily guest limit into 500,000 tokens of web chat a month, on every supported model. Token use depends on the model and the conversation: thinking models and long chats use more.

Signing in also unlocks file uploads, free image generation with GPT Image 2.5 and Nano Banana 2.1, and [projects](/guides/projects). All three need **Server Storage**, the default. The [chat storage guide](/guides/chat-storage) explains the other storage modes.

Free accounts do not include API access.

## What Premium adds

Premium costs USD 8 a month:

- **Unlimited web chat** on every supported model, including GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon.
- **An API up to 92% below official prices.** Premium includes 5M API tokens a month, and top-ups cost USD 8 per 5M tokens of any supported model. One key works with [10 documented coding agents](/guides/one-api-key-coding-agents), including Codex and Claude Code.

[Plans and limits](/guides/plans-and-limits) compares Guest, Free, Premium, and Team side by side.

## Limits to know

- **The guest limit is counted per IP address.** People on the same network share the 5 daily messages.
- **Guests cannot upload files, create images, or use projects.** Sign in to use them.
- **Guest chats are not saved to an account** until you sign in in the same tab.
- **Requests still go through Capriole AI and the selected model's provider,** with or without an account. Read [privacy and data controls](/guides/privacy-and-security) before you share sensitive material.

## How other AI chat apps handle signed-out use

- **ChatGPT.** OpenAI says you can use ChatGPT without an account in supported countries. While logged out, you can have only one conversation at a time, and chats are not saved. OpenAI's model guide says logged-out users do not get GPT-5.6 Sol, and GPT-6 Pro, the Chat option powered by GPT-6 Astra, needs a Pro, Business, or Enterprise plan.
- **Gemini.** Google says some features of the Gemini web app work without signing in. Other features, and saving your activity, need a Google Account.
- **Claude.** Anthropic's help center says new Claude accounts must verify a phone number from a supported location, and there is no way to skip that step.

On Capriole AI, one browser tab without an account reaches GPT, Claude, and Gemini models.
