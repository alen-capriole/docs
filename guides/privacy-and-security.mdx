Capriole AI does not use your chats, files, or API content to train AI models. You choose where your chat history is kept: on your account, only in your browser, or nowhere. The API keeps no request or response content, only usage records such as token counts. You can export every chat, delete chats at any time, and delete your account. Memory, which keeps facts about you across chats, stays off unless you turn it on.

This guide summarizes how your data is handled. The [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) is the full text, and the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) cover plans, limits, and prohibited use.

## Your content is not used for training

Capriole AI does not use your chats, files, or API content to train AI models.

Each request still goes to the AI model provider that answers it: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Z.ai, Moonshot AI, or ByteDance, or a cloud platform that hosts their models. That provider handles the request under its own terms.

## Choose where chat history is kept

| Storage mode | Where chat history is kept | How to choose it |
|---|---|---|
| **Server Storage** (default) | Your account, on Capriole AI's servers | Leave both storage switches off |
| **Local Storage** | Your browser, not our database | Turn on **Enable local storage** |
| **No Storage** | Not saved | Turn on **Enable No Storage** |

Use Server Storage to sync chats across devices, upload files, and create images.

Both switches are in **Account** → **Settings**. In every mode, your messages pass through our servers to the model that answers them, and Capriole AI keeps a usage record. If you report an answer, we receive that answer and your message before it, whatever the mode. The [chat storage guide](/guides/chat-storage) walks through each mode.

<Warning>
Turning on **Enable No Storage** permanently deletes all chat history from the server and this browser once you confirm.
</Warning>

Guest chats are not saved on our servers. A copy stays in your browser tab so it can be saved to your account if you sign in.

## What Capriole AI keeps

| Data | What is kept |
|---|---|
| Account | Your email address, name, and profile picture from sign-in. The account is shared with Capriole Quant. |
| Chats | Messages, attached files, created images, and chat titles, depending on your storage mode |
| Personalization | The name, work, and instructions you add to your account |
| Memory | Short facts about you in your general memory and each project's memory: the ones you add, and the ones Capriole AI saves or updates from your chats while **Memory** is on |

| Usage records | For each chat and API request: time, model, token counts, status, latency, cost, and IP address, plus the key used for API requests. No message content. |
| Payments | Your subscription status and billing identifiers. Stripe processes the payment; we do not receive your card number. |

## Memory

Capriole AI's memory keeps short facts about you, such as where you live or how you like answers, and uses them in your later chats. It is off until you turn it on in **Account** → **Memory**. After each reply, Capriole AI may save new facts you state about yourself, or update saved ones, in the language you wrote them. Only signed-in chats saved with Server Storage use memory, on the web and in the app. Local Storage, No Storage, and guest chats do not, and neither does the API.

Your memory is kept on our servers with your account, in two kinds of lists:

| List | Where you manage it | Which chats use it | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| General memory | **Account** → **Memory** | Every chat. Chats outside projects save new facts here. | Up to 2,400 characters |
| Project memory | [The project's **Memory** tab](/guides/projects#use-project-memory) | Chats in that project, along with your general memory. They save new facts here. | Up to 1,200 characters per project |

In both lists, you can see everything saved, select **Add memory**, edit or delete a memory, or select **Delete all**. **Delete all** in **Account** → **Memory** clears only your general memory; each project's memory is cleared on its own tab. You manage both lists on the web, and chats in the app use them too. When a list is full, neither you nor Capriole AI can add to it until you shorten or delete a memory.

Turning **Memory** off stops Capriole AI from using or updating memory. Your lists stay, and you can still add, edit, or delete memories. Deleting chats, or all chat history, keeps your memory. Deleting a project deletes its memory.

## What the API keeps

The API does not store request or response content. Capriole AI stores API keys and usage records for quota, billing, and support. You can personalize replies in web chat.

## Export your chats

In **Account** → **Settings**, select **Export chat history**. You get a ZIP with every chat as a Markdown file, the files you attached, and the images you generated, plus `conversations.json`, which holds every chat in a format programs can read. Your projects' instructions and files are in a `projects/` folder, and `conversations.json` records each chat's project.

A large history takes a few minutes. The download stays available for 24 hours, and you can export even after a paid plan ends.

To export one chat, open its menu in the sidebar and choose **Export**. A project exports from the project's menu. Local Storage chats download at once, without being stored on our servers.

## Delete chats or your account

- **One chat**: open the chat's menu in the sidebar and choose **Delete**.
- **All chats**: in **Account** → **Settings**, select **Delete all** next to **Delete all chat history**. Your memory stays, and your projects keep their instructions, files, and memory.
- **A project**: open its menu and choose **Delete**. This also deletes its instructions, files, memory, and chats.
- **Memory**: in **Account** → **Memory** or on a project's **Memory** tab, delete one memory or select **Delete all**.
- **Your account**: open **Account**, select **Manage account**, and choose **Delete account** on the **Security** tab. With an active paid subscription, email [info@capriole.ai](mailto:info@capriole.ai) instead. Because the account is shared with Capriole Quant, deleting it also ends your Capriole Quant sign-in.

Deleting a chat, all chat history, a memory, or a project permanently removes it from our servers. Deleted data can remain in database backups for up to 7 days. Local Storage chats stay in your browser until you clear them.

## Common questions

### Does Capriole AI train AI models on my chats?

No. Capriole AI does not use your chats, files, or API content to train AI models. Model providers receive each request and handle it under their own terms.

### Does Capriole AI store my API requests?

No. It keeps no API request or response content, only usage records such as the time, model, and token counts.

### Can I use Capriole AI without saving my chats?

Yes. Turn on **Enable No Storage** for chats that disappear when you leave or refresh the page, or **Enable local storage** to keep history only in your browser.

### Does Capriole AI have memory across chats?

Yes, once you turn it on. **Memory** is off by default; turn it on in **Account** → **Memory**, and Capriole AI may save short facts you state about yourself in signed-in Server Storage chats and use them in later chats. Each project also keeps its own memory. You can see, add, edit, and delete every fact, or turn **Memory** off at any time.

### How do I delete Capriole AI's memory?

In **Account** → **Memory**, point to a memory and select **×**, or select **Delete all**. A project's memory is on the project's **Memory** tab. Deleting chats or turning **Memory** off keeps your saved memory. Deleted memories can remain in database backups for up to 7 days.

### How do I delete my Capriole AI account?

Open **Account**, select **Manage account**, and choose **Delete account** on the **Security** tab. With an active paid subscription, email [info@capriole.ai](mailto:info@capriole.ai) instead. Deleting it also ends your Capriole Quant sign-in, because the two products share one account.

### Where is Capriole AI data stored?

Our main servers and database are in India. Our providers process data in other countries, including the United States.
