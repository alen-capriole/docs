<Update label="October 30, 2026">
## Project files and export

Give a project the files its chats need. On the project page, open the new **Files** tab and select **Add files**, or drop files onto the tab. You can add the same documents and images as chat attachments, up to 40 per project on every plan. Every chat in the project can use them: Capriole AI reads a file when your question needs it. To take a project with you, open its menu and choose **Export** for a ZIP of its chats, instructions, and files. **Export chat history** on your **Account** page now includes your projects' instructions and files. [Learn how to use project files](/guides/projects).

## Projects

Keep related work together. Create a project for a client, a course, or anything you come back to, and its chats sit under it in the sidebar. Write the project's instructions once, and every chat in the project follows them on top of your account instructions; when the two conflict, the project's instructions win. To create a project, point to **Projects** in the sidebar and select **+**. Then start a chat in it, or move a chat in from the chat's menu or by dragging it onto the project. Projects are available on every plan to signed-in users who keep chats on the server. [Learn how to use projects](/guides/projects).

## Export your chat history

Take your chats with you. On your **Account** page, under **Settings**, choose **Export chat history**. Capriole AI prepares a ZIP in the background, so you can leave the page while it works, and the download stays available for 24 hours. The ZIP holds every chat as a Markdown file, with the files you attached and the images you generated beside it, plus `conversations.json` with every chat in a format programs can read. To export one chat, open its menu in the sidebar and choose **Export**. Chats kept in local storage download at once.

## Personalization

Tell Capriole AI what to call you, what best describes your work, and how you like answers, in the new **Personalization** section on your **Account** page. Every chat follows them from your next message, including chats you already have. Instructions can be up to 1,500 characters. They apply to Web Chat and the app, not to the API.

## Guest chats stay after you sign in

Start a chat without an account, then sign in or sign up in the same tab. The chat opens as a saved chat in your account with every message in place, and the next reply remembers what you asked before. Saving it does not count toward your usage.

## Kimi K3.1 Pro

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro is now available. It is Kimi's most capable model, with substantial gains in coding, 3D creation, and financial research, and a 1M-token context window. In Web Chat, choose **Kimi K3.1 Pro Thinking** for harder work or **Kimi K3.1 Pro** for faster replies. Through the API, call `moonshot/kimi-k3.1-pro` on [Chat Completions](/api-reference/endpoint/chat-completions); the [coding agent guides](/integrations/opencode) list it too. Kimi K3 stays available under **More models**.

## Chats remember their model

When you open a saved chat, Capriole AI selects the model of its latest reply, including the Fast or Thinking setting. The choice stays after a refresh, a new sign-in, or on another device. If that model has been retired, your current selection stays.
