## Personalization

Tell Capriole AI what to call you, what best describes your work, and how you like answers, in the new **Personalization** section on your **Account** page. Every chat follows them from your next message, including chats you already have. Instructions can be up to 1,500 characters. They apply to Web Chat and the app, not to the API.

## Guest chats stay after you sign in

Start a chat without an account, then sign in or sign up in the same tab. The chat opens as a saved chat in your account with every message in place, and the next reply remembers what you asked before. Saving it does not count toward your usage.

## Kimi K3.1 Pro

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro is now available. It is Kimi's most capable model, with substantial gains in coding, 3D creation, and financial research, and a 1M-token context window. In Web Chat, choose **Kimi K3.1 Pro Thinking** for harder work or **Kimi K3.1 Pro** for faster replies. Through the API, call `moonshot/kimi-k3.1-pro` on [Chat Completions](/api-reference/endpoint/chat-completions); the [coding agent guides](/integrations/opencode) list it too. Kimi K3 stays available under **More models**.

## Chats remember their model

When you open a saved chat, Capriole AI selects the model of its latest reply, including the Fast or Thinking setting. The choice stays after a refresh, a new sign-in, or on another device. If that model has been retired, your current selection stays.
